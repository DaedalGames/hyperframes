title: "Is HyperFrames free?"
sidebarTitle: "Is it free?"

---

Yes. The HyperFrames framework, CLI, and agent skills are open source under the
[Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/blob/main/LICENSE),
and rendering on your own computer is free. You pay only for the services you choose to add, such as
your AI agent's plan or HeyGen-hosted features.

## What is free

| Part | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| The framework, CLI, and skills | Free and open source (Apache 2.0). You can use, change, and ship them, including in commercial work, under the license terms. |
| Preview and render on your computer | Free. Local rendering does not use HeyGen credits and does not need an account. |
| Offline voice, music, and sound effects | Free. With no key set, voice uses Kokoro, music uses MusicGen, and sound effects come from a bundled library. See [Working offline](/guides/authentication#working-offline). |
| The HyperFrames Studio app | Free to download for Mac and Linux. You do not need to sign in to download it. |
| Studio in the browser | Free. It opens from any project with `npx hyperframes preview`. |

## What can cost money

None of these come from HyperFrames itself. Each one bills through its own account.

| You use | Who bills you |
| --- | --- |
| An AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and others) | The agent's provider, on your own plan or API key. In the Studio app, runs bill to the plan of the account your agent is signed in with. See [Connect your agent](/studio-app/connect-your-agent). |
| HeyGen voices, the HeyGen music library, and avatar presenters | Your HeyGen account. Some of these draw on the account's allowance or credits. |
| Cloud rendering with `hyperframes cloud render` | Your HeyGen account, in credits. 4K renders bill at 1.5×. See [Cloud rendering](/deploy/cloud). |
| The hosted HyperFrames connector in an AI chat | Your HeyGen account and the chat product's plan. See [Create through an AI chat](/guides/mcp). |
| Rendering on your own AWS Lambda or Google Cloud Run | Your cloud provider. See [AWS Lambda](/deploy/aws-lambda) and [Google Cloud Run](/deploy/gcp-cloud-run). |
| Other providers you add a key for, such as ElevenLabs or Gemini | That provider. See [Providers used by agent workflows](/guides/authentication#providers-used-by-agent-workflows). |

Check your HeyGen account's usage before a long or repeated run that uses hosted
features.

## Do I need a HeyGen account?

Not to make and render videos on your computer. You need one for HeyGen-hosted
features, cloud rendering, the hosted connector, and the Studio app, which asks
you to sign in on first launch. Signing in and creating an account are the same
step:

```bash
npx hyperframes auth login
```

See [Authentication and API keys](/guides/authentication).
