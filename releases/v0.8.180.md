# HyperFrames v0.8.180

Released on 2026-10-19.

A slow distributed render chunk can now be rendered in smaller parts and assembled back, so a retry can split it instead of timing out again. Previews restore a reloaded video at the playhead, and snapshots decode phone video faster.

## Features

- **Producer:** Render a distributed chunk as frame sub-ranges ([fcd93ce184](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/fcd93ce1843556be4b95e0e133929c319e549609), [#5916](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5916)).

## Fixes

- **Core:** A paused preview puts a reloaded video back at the playhead ([44ac4c4bbb](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/commit/44ac4c4bbbad1c8727a0cd77b9d664aadc8e18e9), [#5912](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes/pull/5912)).
