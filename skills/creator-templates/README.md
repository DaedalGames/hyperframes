Make a new episode in a top creator's series format, on your topic, in your own voice. Part of HyperFrames Pro.

More templates are coming.

## How to use it

Ask your agent, for example:

> Make a Vox-style video about why American pickup trucks are so big.

It checks your plan, pulls the template you need, writes the script with you and builds the episode as an editable HyperFrames project.

These are format templates. They hold no creator footage, voice or logo, and the channels named here are not affiliated with HyperFrames.
